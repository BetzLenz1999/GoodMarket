  1. Join the open round by signing a 250 G$ participation transaction.

How winners are selected

The raffle is handled by the smart contract, not Supabase. The contract stores the 400 participant wallets, blocks extra entries once full, and only then allows winner selection.

  1. Every 250 G$ participation adds one wallet address to the current round.
  2. When the 400th wallet joins, the contract changes the round to drawing mode and stops accepting more deposits.
  3. The automated raffle keeper submits a fresh randomness seed to the contract.
  4. The contract uses that seed to choose 10 unique wallet addresses from the 400 participants.
  5. Each winning wallet gets a 10,000 G$ claimable reward and must tap Withdraw Reward to receive it.
