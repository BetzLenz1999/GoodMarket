How it works

1Start with at least {{ farming_config.min_farm_gd }} G$. Every {{ farming_config.chicken_price_gd }} G$ buys 1 chicken, so the minimum farm starts with {{ farming_config.min_chickens }} chickens.
2Each chicken produces 1 egg per day during the farming cycle. Egg reward value follows the live contract configuration.
3Sell eggs during the cycle, or wait until maturity. Closing the farm returns principal plus any remaining egg rewards.
4Minimum final withdrawal is hardcoded at {{ farming_config.min_withdraw_gd }} G$ in the smart contract.
5Rewards come from the contract reward pool, funded by admins from the Admin Dashboard. Gas is estimated by the Celo network and your wallet — it is not a hardcoded farming fee.
