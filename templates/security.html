Security first

Protect your wallet

GoodMarket uses approved wallet connections so transaction approval stays with you and your wallet provider.

GoodMarket support will never ask you to reveal, paste, send, or screenshot your wallet recovery phrase or raw signing credentials.

Safe connection rules

  • Check that you are visiting goodmarketph.live before connecting.
  • Review the network, recipient, token, and amount inside your wallet before approving.
  • Reject unexpected signature or transaction requests.
  • Use WalletConnect or another connection shown by your trusted wallet application.
  • Remember that GoodMarket is a community initiative, not an official GoodDollar product.

If something looks suspicious

Disconnect the site in your wallet, reject pending requests, and contact the GoodMarket team through a verified community channel. If wallet secrets may have been exposed, create a new wallet using trusted wallet software and move your assets promptly.

Never accept direct messages claiming that a secret credential is required for support, verification, rewards, or account recovery.
