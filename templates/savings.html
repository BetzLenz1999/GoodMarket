What is GoodMarket Savings?
  • 1
    Pick a Plan (optional) or choose your lock duration by entering any whole number from 1 to 360 days. Plans are friendly goals bound to a duration — e.g. 🔧 House Repair = 360 days, 📚 Education = 180 days. Your tokens go directly into a smart contract on Celo Mainnet.
  • 2
    Each (token · lock days) is a slot. If you top up the same token + duration later, the new amount is added to the existing slot — the unlock date does not change. Different day counts open separate slots.
  • 3
    Funds are frozen until the unlock date — no one (not even GoodMarket) can touch them. Early withdrawal is not allowed; only the depositor can withdraw, and only after maturity.
  • 4
    After the lock ends, click "Withdraw". Your full slot amount (plus any G$ bonus) is returned to your wallet.
  • ★
    Custom-duration preview: Reward estimates update live as you type the amount and lock days.
  • ★
    Custom-duration bonuses: 1–29 days can qualify for 30 G$ at the per-token minimum. 30–360 days can qualify for lock days × 500 / 30 G$ at the 100k G$ equivalent tier.
  • ★
    Loyalty bonus: Locks of at least 300 days can qualify for a flat 20,000 G$ loyalty bonus at the 1M G$ equivalent tier. The reward pool funds payouts best-effort, and withdrawals remain available even when the pool is low.

Smart Contract Security
  • Only the depositing wallet can withdraw its slots — not the platform, not anyone else.
  • The contract has no owner, no admin, no pause, no emergency withdrawal. Reward pool funds can never be withdrawn by anyone — only used to pay saver bonuses.
  • Early withdrawal is not allowed — once locked, your tokens stay locked until the unlock date you committed to.
  • All transactions are publicly visible on CeloScan.
